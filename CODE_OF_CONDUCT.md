# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

Be kind and assume good intent. Keep feedback about the work, and respect other people's time and opinions. Help newcomers find their way.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning language
- Sexual content or unwanted attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Repeatedly disrupting a discussion

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems through a [private report](https://github.com/eljonathas/InstantPad/security/advisories/new) on this repository. Only the maintainer reads it. GitHub's [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools are also available.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, or warn, block or ban anyone who breaks this code, in proportion to what happened.
